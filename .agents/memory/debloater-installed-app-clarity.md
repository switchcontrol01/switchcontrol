---
name: Debloater installed-app clarity
description: Readability and icon-fidelity requirements for the Installed Apps view.
---

Debloater's navigation tabs and Installed Apps filters must remain comfortably readable, high-contrast, and easy to click. Installed-app icons should come from the app itself when possible; never treat generic uninstall/setup icons as the product identity. For known launchers and peripheral apps, prefer official publisher artwork over a generic installer icon.

**Why:** The user reported that the tabs and filters were too small and difficult to read, while Bodycam, FIFINE Control Deck, and Epic Games Launcher showed missing or generic icons.

**How to apply:** When changing this view, check contrast, text size, and hit targets at desktop and narrow widths. When changing icon lookup, cover installer-stub avoidance, cache invalidation, and official publisher sources.
