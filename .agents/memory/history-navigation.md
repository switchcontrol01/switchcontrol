History must not be a separate left-sidebar entry or an available sidebar-customization item. Keep the History page and stored logs, reached through **View Logs** on Dashboard.

**Why:** The user requested a simpler sidebar with History accessible through the Dashboard instead.

**How to apply:** Preserve the History route and Dashboard link when changing navigation; do not restore the sidebar item.
