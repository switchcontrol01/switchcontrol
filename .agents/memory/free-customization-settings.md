All appearance, accessibility, layout, tweak-behavior, notification, startup, and privacy customization controls should remain available to free users.

**Why:** The user explicitly changed the original requirement from premium-gated customization to free customization.

**How to apply:** Do not wrap these controls in entitlement checks or route them through the upgrade modal. Preserve existing premium gates for optimization tools and premium account features.
