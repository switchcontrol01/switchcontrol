TASK
Remove the current intro flash effect and replace it with a fluid premium transition that feels cinematic, smooth, and stable.

PROBLEM
The current flash looks weak and cheap. It is too thin, too abrupt, and does not feel premium.
It hurts the intro instead of elevating it.

GOAL
Remove the flash entirely.
Replace it with a smoother animated handoff from splash/welcome into the app.

The new transition must feel:
- fluid
- premium
- cinematic
- soft
- controlled
- modern

No harsh white flash.
No thin streak.
No abrupt blink.

DESIGN DIRECTION
Replace the flash with this sequence:

1. Splash/logo holds briefly with ambient background glow
2. A soft bloom expands behind the center area
3. Splash content fades and slightly scales down
4. Background glow dissolves into the real app background
5. Authenticated shell appears already settled in final position
6. Page content fades in smoothly

This should feel like one continuous motion, not multiple disconnected animations.

IMPLEMENTATION

1. REMOVE CURRENT FLASH EFFECT
Audit and remove the existing flash implementation completely.
If there is a dedicated component for the flash, remove its visual behavior from the intro flow.

Do not leave any thin streak, hard white blast, or sudden exposure effect.

2. ADD A SOFT BLOOM TRANSITION
Create a large soft radial bloom behind the splash/logo area:
- much wider than the old flash
- low opacity
- blurred heavily
- expands gently
- fades out gradually

This bloom should feel like energy dispersing, not a camera flash.

3. USE OPACITY + SCALE, NOT HARSH LIGHT
For the transition:
- splash container fades from 1 to 0
- splash container scales slightly down, around 1.0 -> 0.985 or similar
- optional tiny upward drift only if it stays elegant
- app background fades in underneath
- no blur spikes, no strobing, no brightness hit

4. KEEP PREMIUM BACKGROUND FEEL
Preserve:
- spotlight / ambient glows
- noise texture
- dark premium atmosphere
- depth
- glass feeling

The replacement transition should merge naturally into these existing visuals.

5. AUTHENTICATED APP MUST NOT RE-ANIMATE THE SHELL
After the splash/welcome transition finishes:
- app shell must already be in final position
- no second snap
- no relayout
- no post-transition shift

Only content inside the app may animate.

6. KEEP MOTION, REMOVE CHEAPNESS
Do not make it static.
The replacement still needs motion.
But it must be elegant motion:
- bloom
- fade
- scale
- dissolve
- soft reveal

7. TEST FLOW
Test:
- splash -> welcome -> authenticated
- splash -> authenticated directly
- no visual jump
- no double load feel
- no abrupt exposure effect

SUCCESS CRITERIA
- old flash completely removed
- transition feels smoother and more premium
- no harsh streak or blink
- intro feels fluid and cinematic
- app enters naturally
- shell remains stable after transition

CHECKLIST
- remove flash effect
- replace with soft bloom transition
- use fade/scale dissolve
- preserve premium glows/background depth
- prevent shell snap after intro
- verify smooth handoff into final app state

FINAL OUTPUT
Return:
1. what old flash code was removed
2. what new transition replaced it
3. files changed
4. confirmation that shell stability was preserved
