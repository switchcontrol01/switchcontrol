## 1. The `loadFile()` rejection at startup — likely benign, but worth confirming

```

```

This happens at `07:08:38.671Z`, right in the middle of the auth deep-link flow. Looking at the surrounding context, this is almost certainly a race: the deep-link (`switchcontrol://auth/callback?...`) arrived and got processed *before* `loadFile()` finished resolving, and something in that handling (likely a redirect or navigation triggered by the auth callback) interrupted the initial file load. Worth a quick check: does `did-fail-load` have any recovery/retry logic, or does it just log and move on? If it's pure logging with no fallback, that's a latent risk worth hardening even though it didn't bite this time.
