TASK

Fix the full first-time user transition chain so it feels cinematic, fluid, intentional, and slow enough to read as premium.

PROBLEM

The new-user flow is visually broken.

Current issues:

1. After login, the app transitions into the Welcome animation too abruptly.
2. The fade-out / blur-out and fade-in / blur-in are either missing, too weak, or too fast.
3. The Welcome animation contains a random lightning bolt visual that feels out of place and should be removed.
4. The final "You're all set" screen does not exit smoothly into the dashboard.
5. The dashboard and app shell do not properly blur in and fade in after the intro completes.
6. The entire sequence feels rushed instead of cinematic.

This is not a small polish issue.
It breaks the premium feel of the whole onboarding flow.

GOAL

Make the entire first-time user flow feel like one connected premium sequence:

Login success
→ Welcome intro enters smoothly
→ Welcome content plays cleanly
→ "You're all set" screen exits smoothly
→ Dashboard / full app shell enters with a visible, elegant blur-and-fade reveal

Everything must feel:

- smooth
- premium
- slow enough to register
- intentional
- emotionally satisfying

IMPLEMENTATION

1. Audit the full transition chain

Inspect the full first-time flow across:

- login success state
- phase changes into welcome
- WelcomeAnimation mount animation
- final "You're all set" screen
- phase change from welcome to authenticated
- dashboard/app shell enter animation

Find all places where:

- transitions are too short
- transitions overlap badly
- one screen disappears before the next is visually ready
- blur values are too weak
- opacity timing is too abrupt

2. Remove the lightning bolt from the Welcome animation

There is a visual lightning bolt / electric accent in the welcome sequence.
Remove it entirely.

Do not replace it with another gimmick.
Keep the welcome screen cleaner and more premium.

3. Strengthen login success → welcome transition

When login succeeds:

- login screen must blur out and fade out more clearly
- welcome screen must fade in and blur in more clearly
- there must be a soft overlap, not a hard handoff

Required feel:

- outgoing screen dissolves
- incoming screen resolves into focus

Target direction:

- outgoing blur: around 18px to 30px
- incoming blur: around 14px to 20px
- transition duration: around 1.1s to 1.6s
- ease: premium smooth curve, not linear

4. Slow down welcome entrance

The welcome screen itself should not appear instantly sharp.

It should:

- come in slightly soft
- settle into focus
- give the logo and text room to breathe

The user must feel like they have entered a special sequence, not just another page mount.

5. Fix "You're all set" exit

At the end of the intro tour / welcome flow:

- the "You're all set" screen must fade out smoothly
- the "You're all set" screen must blur out smoothly
- the dashboard must not appear too early underneath
- the dashboard must not snap in hard

This is the most important handoff in the whole sequence.

Required behavior:

- final welcome screen holds briefly
- then dissolves out with blur
- then dashboard/app shell resolves in with matching blur-in and fade-in

6. Strengthen dashboard/app shell entry after intro

After welcome completes:

- the entire app shell must fade in
- the entire app shell must blur in
- not just pieces of the dashboard
- not just cards
- the whole app frame should feel like it is materializing

That includes:

- sidebar
- top banners
- dashboard hero
- main content container

Use one coordinated enter animation at the authenticated app level.

7. Increase transition timing across the full onboarding chain

Current transitions feel too short and too rushed.

Adjust to premium pacing.

Target pacing guidance:

- login success hold before next phase: longer than current
- welcome enter: around 1.2s to 1.8s effective feel
- final welcome exit: around 1.0s to 1.4s
- app shell enter: around 0.9s to 1.3s

Important:
Do not make it sluggish.
Make it elegant and readable.

8. Ensure transitions are chained, not fighting

Do not let:

- old screen disappear instantly
- new screen appear before old screen has visually exited
- welcome and app both fight for opacity at the same time
- background snap independently from foreground

Use one coordinated sequence where each stage finishes properly before the next fully resolves.
