CONTEXT: WHY THIS REDESIGN IS HAPPENING

This is not a cosmetic refresh. The current website has serious perception and structural problems that must be fixed.

CURRENT PROBLEMS

1) The site looks “AI generated”
- Generic layout patterns
- Default spacing and card grids
- No strong hierarchy
- No visual depth model
- No intentional lighting or material logic
- Repetitive sections that feel templated

2) It feels flat and 2D
- No layered background system
- No consistent glass material
- No light source logic
- No depth stack (foreground / midground / background)
- No contour, noise, glow, or atmosphere layers

3) Weak emotional impact
- No cinematic hero stage
- No dramatic reveal motion
- No visual anchors
- CTAs do not feel premium
- No tension, build-up, or visual progression

4) Motion feels cheap or inconsistent
- Default transitions
- Sections appear without intention
- Scroll feels abrupt in places
- No unified motion language
- No reduced-motion handling

5) Pages are inconsistent
- Different spacing logic per page
- Different card styles
- Different shadows
- No single layout system
- No single component system

6) Mobile experience is not premium
- Visuals feel compressed
- Some animations do not adapt properly
- No clear mobile-first thought process
- No structured QA across all routes

7) The brand does not feel like a $20,000 product
The goal is to create a website that:
- Feels intentional
- Feels engineered
- Has depth, glow, subtle luxury
- Feels premium but controlled
- Feels like a serious product, not a template

WHY WE ARE DOING THIS

We are elevating the product positioning.

SwitchControl is not a hobby project. The website must:
- Signal quality immediately in 3 seconds
- Feel original
- Feel technically competent
- Feel engineered and premium
- Maintain performance and responsiveness

This redesign is about:
- Cohesion
- Emotional impact
- Depth and material realism
- Motion discipline
- Design system integrity

WHAT MUST NOT HAPPEN

- Do not apply random glows everywhere.
- Do not add decorative clutter.
- Do not make everything animate at once.
- Do not introduce inconsistent spacing.
- Do not add new pages unless they already exist.
- Do not change content meaning.

WHAT SUCCESS LOOKS LIKE

When finished:
- Every route looks like it belongs to the same system.
- The hero stage feels cinematic.
- Depth is obvious but subtle.
- Cards feel like floating glass panels with controlled light.
- Motion feels engineered.
- Mobile feels deliberate, not compressed.
- No page feels like a template.

This is a full structural elevation, not a theme swap.
Treat it as rebuilding the experience layer of the product.
