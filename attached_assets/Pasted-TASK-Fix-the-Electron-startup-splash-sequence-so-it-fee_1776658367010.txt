TASK

Fix the Electron startup splash sequence so it feels like one directed cinematic animation instead of multiple background systems loading in layers.

PROBLEM

The current startup feels wrong because the background appears before the logo and text feel established.

The main causes are:

1. Two background systems are active during splash:
   
   - "Splash.tsx" already renders its own full-screen atmospheric scene
   - "App.tsx" also renders a persistent animated background during "phase === "splash""

2. Foreground content is delayed too much:
   
   - logo starts at 320ms
   - text starts at 1000ms

3. There is no single directed reveal layer controlling the visual sequence.

4. Splash timing is split between parent and child:
   
   - "Splash.tsx" has internal completion timing
   - "App.tsx" separately controls splash completion with external timers
   - this creates drift and makes the sequence feel stitched together

GOAL

Make startup feel premium and intentional.

Desired sequence:

1. dark base exists instantly
2. logo fades and de-blurs in first
3. title and tagline follow quickly
4. subtle particles are already alive in the background
5. a diagonal top-to-bottom background sweep/glow reveals after the logo is visible
6. all remaining ambient effects finish softly behind the content

Do not remove the current visual style.
Do not simplify the design.
Keep the existing aesthetic, but fix sequencing, ownership, and timing.

IMPLEMENTATION

1. Remove duplicate background ownership during splash

In "App.tsx", the persistent background layer must NOT render during "phase === "splash"".

Current logic includes splash:

- "splash"
- "unauthenticated"
- "login_success"
- "welcome"

Change it so the persistent background only renders for:

- "unauthenticated"
- "login_success"
- "welcome"

Reason:
"Splash.tsx" already owns the full-screen scene during splash.
There must only be one background owner during startup.

2. Make foreground appear first

In "Splash.tsx", reduce the delays for logo and text.

Current:

- logo at 320ms
- text at 1000ms

Change to something closer to:

- logo at 60ms to 100ms
- text at 180ms to 260ms

The logo and title must establish the brand before the environment fully blooms.

3. Add a real reveal sweep layer

Inside "Splash.tsx", add one dedicated animated diagonal reveal layer above the haze layers but below the logo/text.

This sweep should:

- begin only after the logo is visible
- move diagonally from top-left toward bottom-right
- feel soft and atmospheric
- not look like a hard wipe
- use blur, low opacity, and premium glow colors already used in the splash

This is not a replacement for the current glow layers.
It is a sequencing layer that tells the eye where to look and when.

4. Keep particles subtle at the beginning

Do not add chaotic particles.

Keep the current particle system but make sure:

- particles are already present at low opacity at startup
- they do not overpower the logo
- they gradually feel more visible after the foreground appears

5. Reduce late visual interruptions

Review "CameraGlow" timing in "App.tsx".

Right now it starts later in the startup window and may be contributing to the stitched feeling.

Make sure it does not create a second visual wake-up moment after the splash is already underway.

If needed:

- reduce its strength
- move it to exit timing only
- or disable it during splash if it overlaps badly
