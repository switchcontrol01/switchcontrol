**2. The audio glitch is almost certainly the PowerShell/process burst, not the cloud wait itself**

Audio glitches (crackling, stutter, dropouts) on Windows are a classic symptom of **CPU scheduling pressure** — something is either pegging a core or causing enough process-spawn churn that the audio engine's real-time thread misses its deadline. Each PowerShell spawn carries real OS overhead (process creation, CLR/PowerShell host startup), and a cluster of them firing close together is a very plausible audio-glitch trigger even though no single one looks slow in isolation.
