Do not add vague "optimization percentage" controls.

• NaN
• null
• undefined
• negative values
• out-of-range values
• invalid history entries
• broken rollbacks
• trial expiration failures
• fake "applied" states
• duplicate tweak IDs
• orphaned executor mappings
