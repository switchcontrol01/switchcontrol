TASK

Redesign the sign-out transition so it feels fluid, premium, and cinematic instead of instantly hard-cutting to the login screen.

PROBLEM

When clicking the sign out button, the app immediately cuts to the login screen.

Current behavior feels abrupt and cheap:

* no transition
* no smooth state change
* no visual continuity
* app just instantly disappears into login

This breaks the premium feel of the app.

GOAL

Make sign-out transition smooth and intentional.

Desired result:

* click sign out
* app softly blurs/dims/fades
* transition flows nicely into the login screen
* login screen fades/blurs in smoothly
* whole process feels premium and polished

TARGET TIMING

Total transition should feel smooth and controlled:

* target around 2.0 to 3.0 seconds total
* around 1.2 to 1.5 seconds for app fade/blur out
* smooth handoff into login screen
* final login settle animation should feel clean, not abrupt

IMPORTANT

Do NOT fake this by simply delaying sign-out with a timeout.
Do NOT leave the app interactive while fading out.
Do NOT hard-cut auth state before the visual transition is ready.

Fix the transition architecture properly.

IMPLEMENTATION

1. ADD CONTROLLED SIGN-OUT TRANSITION STATE

Create a transition state such as:

* isSigningOut
* signOutTransitionStage

Suggested stages:

* idle
* signout_requested
* app_fading_out
* auth_switch
* login_fading_in
* complete

2. PREVENT IMMEDIATE HARD UNMOUNT

Right now sign-out likely invalidates auth immediately and causes the full app tree to switch at once.

Instead:

* start visual sign-out transition first
* temporarily lock interactions
* fade/blur app shell out
* switch auth/login view at the right point
* then animate login screen in

3. APP EXIT ANIMATION

Animate the signed-in app UI out as one coordinated scene:

* soften brightness slightly
* apply subtle blur
* fade content smoothly
* reduce motion/contrast slightly
* maintain visual stability, no snapping

This should affect:

* sidebar
* main content
* top-level app shell

The app should feel like it is gracefully closing its active session.

4. LOGIN SCREEN ENTRY

Bring the login screen in smoothly:

* fade in
* slight blur-to-sharp settle
* gentle scale/position settle if appropriate
* no sudden pop

The login panel should feel like it belongs to the same visual world as the app, not a separate hard cut.

5. NO WHITE FLASH / NO BLACK FLASH / NO EMPTY FRAME

Ensure there is:

* no intermediate blank frame
* no raw background flash
* no hard auth repaint visible
* no jump between shells

6. BUTTON FEEDBACK

When clicking sign out:

* button should acknowledge click immediately
* show non-interactive transition state
* prevent double click or repeated sign-out triggers

7. KEEP IT SMOOTH IN ELECTRON

Use transform/opacity/filter-based transitions where possible.
Avoid layout-shifting animation.
Avoid remount flicker.

8. TEST BOTH STATES

Test:

* sign out from dashboard
* sign out from other pages
* sign out while modal/tour is not active
* sign out while app has loaded telemetry
* confirm no glitches in packaged Electron app

DESIGN DIRECTION

The transition should feel:

* premium
* fluid
* polished
* cinematic
* controlled

Not:

* slow
* flashy
* overdone
* fake

FINAL OUTPUT REQUIRED

Return:

* files changed
* transition state logic used
* exact sign-out animation flow
* timing breakdown
* confirmation there is no instant hard cut anymore
* confirmation it was tested in the packaged app
