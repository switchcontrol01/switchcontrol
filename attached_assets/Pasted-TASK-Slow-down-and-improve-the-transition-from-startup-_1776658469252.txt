- the welcome screen exits too quickly
- the dashboard fades and de-blurs in too quickly
- the transition does not have enough atmospheric hold
- the result feels like a polished fast swap instead of a premium cinematic handoff

GOAL

- slower
- more deliberate
- more atmospheric
- more cinematic
- more satisfying

The transition should feel like:

1. welcome screen dissolves and blurs away with weight
2. a soft atmospheric handoff happens
3. dashboard emerges from haze
4. dashboard settles into clarity
