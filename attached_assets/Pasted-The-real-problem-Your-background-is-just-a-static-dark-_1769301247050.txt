The real problem

Your background is just a static dark gradient, so every page feels like the same dead panel.

You need:

Depth

Motion

Focus

Energy
Without hurting readability.

What to add (the best combo)
1) “Living” background layer (subtle, always moving)

This should exist on every page.

Add:

2 to 4 slow moving glow blobs (purple, cyan, blue)

very low opacity

blurred heavy

movement like drifting

This makes the UI feel alive instantly.

Do NOT make it bright. It should be felt, not seen.

2) Topography / contour layer (only slightly visible)

Yes. This is your brand style already.

Add:

topographic contour lines

5% opacity max

slow parallax movement on scroll

and a tiny shimmer sweep every 6 to 10 seconds

This makes it look “high tech” without looking childish.

3) Micro particles (ONLY in 1 place)

Particles everywhere = cheap.

Use particles in ONLY:

login screen background

or the dashboard hero header area

Keep them:

slow

few

soft glow

not moving fast

4) Shines and highlights (only on interactive things)

You want premium “feel” on hover/click.

Add shine effects to:

buttons

toggles

cards

sliders

nav icons

Examples:

hover = soft glow + lift

click = quick “press in” scale + ripple

focus = glowing outline

Animations on every click (yes, but smart)

You don’t want random animations. You want feedback.

Must-have click animations

Buttons

press down (scale 0.98)

glow pulse

tiny shimmer sweep

Cards

hover lift + border glow

click = subtle “snap” feedback

Toggles

smooth spring

glow color shift when enabled

Sliders

thumb glow + trail

value pop animation

Page transitions

fade + slight slide up

NOT full screen wipe (too much)

Add this ONE thing to make it feel 10x more expensive
“Spotlight follow” effect

When you move your mouse, the background has a soft radial glow that follows.

desktop only

super subtle

makes it feel like a real app

This alone makes it feel like a $99 tool.
