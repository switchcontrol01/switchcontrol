- grouped controls
- timeline-first orchestration

- startup sequence
- immediate launch apps
- delayed launch apps
- protected/core apps
- estimated boot impact distribution
- category groupings
- maybe staged launch bands

Need:
- fluid animated timeline
- clean horizontal sequence visualization
- app chips / blocks placed in launch order
- smooth transitions when delay state changes
- better readability and hierarchy
- premium glass/timeline treatment

This should feel like a true signature feature.
